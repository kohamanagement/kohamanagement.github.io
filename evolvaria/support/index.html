Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Open a request on GitHub. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and which age and stage you were on. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move to a new phone.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore next to "Bought before?" under "Ads and privacy". Ads you choose to watch (to keep your points after a death) stay available; nothing else plays ads.

There is no sound.
Check the volume bars in the game's Settings. If your iPhone's silent switch is on, set "Phone on silent" to Play in the game's Settings.

How do I change ad privacy?
In the game, Settings, "Ad privacy choices", or iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. See the privacy policy.

A stage is too hard.
You can lower the difficulty in the game's Settings at any time. Evolving new traits between stages, or finding a hidden road on the map, often makes a stage easier.
