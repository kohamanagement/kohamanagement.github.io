Privacy policy

Evolvaria: Cell to Mind, for iPhone. Last updated November 23, 2026.

This policy explains what information the Evolvaria app ("the game") uses and why. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you and has no accounts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those ads.

What the game keeps

Your progress (stages, traits, fossils and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. We run no servers, no accounts and no analytics of our own. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad now and then between ages and after a boss, and offers ads you can choose to watch (for example, to keep your points after a death). These ads come from Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the game asks;
  • other device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, IP address and a coarse location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and basic crash and performance information from the ads software.

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just less personal ones. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game first shows Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, "Ad privacy choices". Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can stop the ads between ages.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission: iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Ad consent (where offered): in the game, Settings, "Ad privacy choices".
  • No more ads between ages: buy "Remove ads" in the game's Settings.
  • Your saved progress: delete the app to delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy: open a request on GitHub.
